# Quiz

1. Read `progress.md`: completed steps, and "Weak spots to revisit".
2. Ask **five** questions, one at a time, waiting for each answer:
   - at least two on recorded weak spots,
   - at least two that require applying a concept to a new situation (e.g. "your agent loops forever on X; which two mechanisms would you use and why?"),
   - one "spot the bug" question with a short code snippet that uses a deprecated or incorrect pattern.
3. After each answer, give direct feedback: what was right, what was missing, and the one-sentence correct model.
4. Update "Weak spots to revisit" in `progress.md`: add concepts the learner missed and remove those they've now answered well twice.
